# Shoreline animal · jaws 1, legs 2

An intermediate jaw specialisation between the [normal shoreline animal](../EVO_shore_base_v1/README.md) and the [large-jawed predator](../EVO_shore_bite_tier3_v1/README.md). Short, visible conical teeth in both jaws, a gently parted mouth and a slightly firmer lower jaw show the first biting upgrade. Its flattened head and restrained teeth leave a clear step up to the large-jawed form.

The requested phenotype is **legs 2, jaws 1, head armour 0**. All four developed supporting legs and webbed feet follow the normal shoreline reference. The elongated body, swimming tail, lateral line, golden eye, dark slate-olive back and warm sandy belly preserve the same species and drawing style.

`masters/shore_bite1_legs2.png` is the unmodified transparent PNG master: one complete animal facing right in side view, with no scenery, text, stats or cast shadow. Dimensions, visible bounds, references and SHA-256 checksum are recorded in `manifest.json`.
