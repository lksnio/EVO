# Shoreline animal · head armour 1

A lighter skull-only variation of the [head-armour-two shoreline animal](../EVO_shore_skull_tier2_v1/README.md). Thinner, closely fitted forehead bones and small temple supports form a clear first-stage shield. The large cheek buttresses are reduced to expose natural scaled skin around the cheek and mouth.

All four legs, webbed feet, stance, body proportions, swimming tail, base jaws, golden eye and slate-olive back / sandy belly follow the reference. Only the strength and coverage of the cranial armour are reduced to level one.

`masters/shore_skull_tier1.png` is the unmodified generated transparent PNG master: one complete animal in side view facing right, with no scenery, text, stats or cast shadow. Dimensions, visible bounds, source reference and SHA-256 checksum are recorded in `manifest.json`.

Art master for later integration. Evolution rules, combat values, runtime textures and animation registration are unchanged.
