# Starter fish · first head-armour specialisation

A gentle, visible cranial shield for the [latest base fish](../EVO_startfish_base_v2/README.md). Thin, warm grey-beige forehead bones and smaller rear support plates follow the existing head contour. Natural sutures and a slightly firmer frontal surface communicate the emerging protection and ramming specialisation.

The [armoured shoreline animal](../EVO_shore_skull_tier3_v1/README.md) is the user's level-one visual armour reference for this design. Its broad organic plates establish the same design family. The fish retains the base mouth, jaw, eye, small fins, tail, lateral line, body proportions and dark slate-olive back / sandy belly.

`masters/startfish_g0_f0_k1.png` is the unmodified generated transparent PNG: one complete fish in side view facing right, with clear margins and no background, text, stats or cast shadow. Dimensions, visible bounds, source references and SHA-256 checksum are recorded in `manifest.json`.

Art master for later integration. Game rules, combat values, runtime textures and animation registration are unchanged.
